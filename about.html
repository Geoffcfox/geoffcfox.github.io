About me

I started in front of the camera. I belong behind it.

I started in social media, on screen and in front of the camera. I took some time to find myself, and what I learned was pretty clear: I would rather be behind the screen than in front of it. That is what led me into marketing, design, photo, and video.

I earned a B.S. in Business, with a concentration in Digital Marketing, from Full Sail University, and I graduated as Valedictorian. Today I am a Marketing Coordinator II at Turner Construction in Philadelphia. There I put together RFP and RFQ books, collateral, signage, and newsletters, and I photograph and film projects as they go up.

I work in Adobe, Canva, and Figma, and I also draw in Fresco. I am based in Philadelphia, PA, and I am open to relocating.

  • Also: Habitat for Humanity volunteer, Cambodia; varsity captain in lacrosse and soccer, ten varsity letters.
