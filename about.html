About

A path through rooms that had to run on time.

I came to marketing through operations and through the jobsite. At Equinox in New York I ran a thirty-person front desk team and designed a training program that cut onboarding time by 30 percent. Earlier, as a VDC intern at Turner Construction, I wrote the guides and walked the site. That habit — make it clear, make it useful, get it there on deadline — is still how I approach a campaign or a proposal.

I now work as a Marketing Coordinator at Turner Construction in Philadelphia. I build RFP and RFQ books, collateral, signage, newsletters, and interview materials, and I photograph and film the life of a project as it goes up. The through-line is the same: help a team walk into a room looking like they already belong there.

I hold a B.S. in Digital Marketing from Full Sail University, where I graduated as Valedictorian and received the Course Director Award for Leadership & Organization. I live in New York City and I’m open to relocating.

  • Also: Habitat for Humanity volunteer, Cambodia; varsity captain in lacrosse and soccer, ten varsity letters.
